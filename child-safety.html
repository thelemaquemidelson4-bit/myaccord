Myaccord Child Safety Standards Myaccord is committed to protecting children and preventing child sexual abuse and exploitation on our platform. Zero Tolerance Policy Myaccord has zero tolerance for child sexual abuse material, child exploitation, grooming, sexual solicitation of minors, or any content that endangers children. Reporting Users can report content, accounts, messages, or behavior that may violate our child safety standards directly through the Myaccord application. Reports involving possible child abuse or exploitation will be reviewed and handled as quickly as possible. Enforcement Myaccord may remove content, restrict accounts, suspend users, or permanently ban accounts that violate these child safety standards. Cooperation With Authorities Myaccord complies with applicable child protection laws and will cooperate with relevant law enforcement or child protection authorities when required.
